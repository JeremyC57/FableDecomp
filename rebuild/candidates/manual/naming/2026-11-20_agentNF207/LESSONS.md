# NF207 lessons (one line each)
- For the u64 combatant map, read the victim's entry through the inline GetCombatantInfo(const CThingCreatureBase&) reference: a named const_iterator lets VC7.1 sink a MFIND_NOINLINE _M_find call below the next branch (0x006E9700: 146v145 -> RELOCATION_MATCH).
- When a row's expression `X.find(k)->second.M.Call(arg)` needs `arg` pushed before the lookup (as a node*-returning stand-in gave), every std::map spelling evaluates the lookup first: that is the 0x006E93C0 residue, not a frame-slot issue.
- A row whose HEAD insert was opaque (declared only) ports straight onto fable_map.h's declared-only insert_unique; a row whose HEAD insert stand-in had a same-TU body needs FABLE_RETAIL_STLP_MAP's visible insert_unique, which emits pick-any copies (insert_unique, operator--, _M_decrement): cmpbody every one.
- A pick-any copy the owner row builds under a row-head `#pragma optimize("s")` cannot be reproduced by a pragma around an out-of-class template member definition (the instance keeps the TU's /O2): 0x006EDA80 _M_decrement 57 v 64.
- A "same type" cast at the call (`find((unsigned __int64)id)`) is folded away; a signed __int64 local is what forces the per-call converted key temporary with the id kept in a register pair (0x006EA050 draft).
- A stand-in self row may be a PDB overload of its own callee: 0x006EA050 (CheckSummonerChargeable) calls IsDefenderDueForAttack(const CCombatantInfo&) and is the (const CThingCreatureBase&) overload in both PDBs; check overload sets of the callee names before trusting a made-up self name.
