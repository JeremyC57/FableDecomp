- The q4492 stand-in family moves in place in one grade per row: delete the row-local CBaseIntelligentPointer / CIntelligentPointer stand-ins, include engine/CIntelligentPointer.h, type the member CIntelligentPointer<CThing>, and declare `template <class T> bool __fastcall IS_THING_ALIVE(const T&);` (declaration only, so no pick-any copy of 0x004CC340 is emitted).
