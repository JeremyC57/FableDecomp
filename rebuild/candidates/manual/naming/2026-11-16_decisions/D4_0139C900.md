**Why:**
- **One name per address.** The owner rule of 2026-11-10 says a name not found in any source may be made up, but must be used

  (g_pchNil, g_pDebugBuffer, g_pchNil_0139c900, g_logBuffer_0139c900, ...).

- **It is already the shared header's spelling.** engine/CString.h declares it, and CString::Init reads it. It was also the
  largest existing family (40 rows).
- **No byte changes.** Every respelling grades as before (RELOCATION_MATCH).
