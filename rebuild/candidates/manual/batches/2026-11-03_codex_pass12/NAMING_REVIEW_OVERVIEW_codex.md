# Naming recovery and remaining identity limits

The exact selected symbol for every authorized function is in STATUS.tsv and its compiler receipt. Each row's NAMING_EVIDENCE.md records the original-name and native-role evidence; donor PDB addresses are never treated as retail addresses. The descriptive input labels are retained only in scope/ROWS.tsv.

Examples of recovered or corroborated identities and source names:

- 00821010 is CMap::LoadBrushesFromFile(CAFile&). Original serialized class/member names, complete sizes and unaligned native accesses establish the file-record layout. Its one approved packing scope and the limits of proof are documented separately.
- 00ae2e20 is CParticleProgram::OptimiseSpringOrder. Original procedure/type records preserve the unusual local spelling egde_distances, unsigned particle indices, the original CArray/set element types and the spring record fields. The selected full function and all20 operands match. The extra historical0051ac70 naming claim was corrected: that separate helper destroys CMapDesc objects containing a signed set, not bare set objects.
- 0051fd80 is the private CThingManager::LoadSingleThing(CPersistContext&,long). Original locals include name, thing_info, sub_script, load_it, pdef, pout_thing, create_it, uid, level_less, ptemp_thing, is_serialising_hero_state, parser, phero and pclass. Its exact bytes remain held because two real STLport helper manglings depend on an unretained extension configuration.
- 00b2d9f0 is CEngineSurfaceCompositionManager::UpdateDiskCache. Original owner, graphic-bank, cache, texture and component field/type/virtual declarations were checked against native offsets and RTTI. Original locals such as update_count, finish_later, start_t, c_morph, surface_cache, graphic_index, graphic_info, elapsed and delta_t are documented. The added temporary names open_flags and filter_key are reconstruction choices, not claimed original names. The complete function and supporting find body match, but the latter's member-template versus non-template spelling is held.
- Original access matters even where bytes do not change: CDefPointer::Object, CIVCountedPointeeBase::IVRefCount, CResource storage and CScriptBase's six storage members are private. The final selected guarded headers restore this while retaining prior defaults.
- The original CVolumeTextureFileInfo misspelling UseageFlags and its21-byte layout are preserved. The layout is proved; the original build-option text is not recovered. This row is parked, and its earlier closer candidate with an unsupported stronger inline annotation is rejected.

The three exact-code naming holds are0051fd80,00520d00 and00b2d9f0. Identical code does not distinguish the STLport extension configurations. Do not choose one merely to silence a binding checker.

GFMain00402510 retains eight explicitly descriptive native-only names whose original spellings were not recovered. They are enumerated in its MAIN_REVIEW.json explicit_unproven_names and row evidence. Its accepted byte/operand result does not claim those names are recovered; retain that qualification when reviewing the permitted descriptive-name fallback.

The CRT symbols __alloca_probe and __chkstk are a proved exception to the usual one-name rule: original RTM chkstk.obj defines both at the same offset, and the entire61-byte helper matches native00bfea30. Neither alias is a wrong spelling. The corresponding reciprocal NAME_FIXES entries were removed.

NAME_FIXES.tsv is a reviewable set of corrections, not a rename script. Final aggregation contains no unresolved address/symbol conflicts. Old contradictory records in history are superseded by the selected row evidence, the final conflict-resolution reports and the current NAME_FIXES table.
