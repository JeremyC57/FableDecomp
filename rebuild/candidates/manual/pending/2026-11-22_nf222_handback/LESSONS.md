- Compare pick-any copies before the final vland run: a port that turns a declared member into the header's inline body (DeleteData in Reset, the converting operator= in a frame-pointer TU) can emit a NEQ copy while the row still grades RELOCATION_MATCH; a declaration-only opt-in restores HEAD's symbol set.
- Build the owner map for pick-any comparison from the post-change spellings (rows' Leaf lines, thunks found by an E9 scan), never from today's reloc_bindings: its ??1<T> lines still name the 44-byte ReleaseData bodies, so every new 5-byte ??1 copy reads NEQ.

- The unlanded operator= owner sits right after the ??1 thunk (0x00429BF9 + 5 = 0x00429BFE ??4<CMeshDataBank>, calls ShareData 0x004291B8): look next to the thunk before calling a copy NO-OWNER.
- A const self fix (QBE) is cheap when the callers' local models declare the callee: `AcquireFont() const` in place moves the binder with no byte change; census callers first (callers.py + absrefs.py).
