## The mechanism (c2.dll 13.10.3077)

- c2 gives every constant in the function a candidate record first (ids 1..N).
- It frees the unused ones by walking a 64-bucket table at 0x10882050 (walk at 0x1077FF3B, hash at 0x1077E10B):
  - an integer constant hashes by `value & 0x3F`;
  - an address constant hashes by the **c1 symbol ordinal & 0x3F** of the symbol it names;
  - inside a bucket, newest first.
- Root candidates (`this`, user locals in first-reference order, then compiler temporaries) take those freed records last-freed-first, then fresh ids.
- Split passes run in ascending record id and pop the free list; reloads at a region entry come out in piece-id order.

So the ids of `this`, the locals and the cleanup-flags temporary depend on how many symbols the TU declared before each global, vftable and string literal the function references. Include order, header growth and extern placement all move it. A dummy function in front of the row changes it too.

## Row 1: 0x005AC10E ConstructActualBuyInventoryPC

**Shape and why.** At 0x005ACB58 retail reloads `mov esi,[ebp+74h]` (children reference) before `mov edi,[ebp+4Ch]` (pframe); the two are reloaded there because the inline text compares clobber esi and edi.

- **It does not share the 0x0059FEE3 structure.** I read the landed twin: its tell is a jcc entering the outer else one instruction past its head after a hoisted common push. Here `je 0x5ACB5E` at 0x005ACAD5 skips the pair and `jmp 0x5ACB58` at 0x005AC9B1 lands on it. That is ordinary threading of the inline `operator==` null-data path. The three SetName arms are a plain if / else-if chain.
- **The cause is a count.** Before the first split pass 17 records are free: `endit`, one inline local, 13 load-CSE temps, one forwarded copy, one temp. Exactly 17 candidates with ids below `ui_trading_goods_list` take one each: constants 0, 1, 2, -1, -8; four string addresses; ptc_shop; a `buyable` field; pviewport; rTextBank; the cleanup-flags temporary; pframe; pmanager; plist_of_goods.
- `ui_trading_goods_list` and the children reference come 18th and 19th and get fresh ids #124 and #125, above pframe's edi piece. Retail has at least two more free records, or two fewer takers.
- **Proven:** `c2_two_more_free_records_proof.cpp` adds named end iterators to the two NotInStock loops. That gives 19 free records, children takes #47, pframe #53, and the pair at 0x005ACB58 comes out in retail's order. The two NotInStock loops then differ instead, because the cached end is stored and compared.
- **The ordinal lever does not apply here.** A uniform shift of 1..63 gives byte-identical output each time; the children reference always gets a fresh id.
- **The Sell sibling (0x005A4CDD) reloads the other way round** at 0x005A57A0 (`edi` frame, then `esi` children), and its trace agrees (pFrame #32, entries #141). So the Buy function really does have two more free records than its twin, and the Buy-only code (the NotInStock loops, `same_categories`) is the likely source.
