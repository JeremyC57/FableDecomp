# NF237 lessons (one line each)
- A Codex NAME_FIXES line is often already true in the table (the Codex rows bind it); the work is the OTHER spellings at the address: census the table's witness column, not just the self leaf.
- A row-local model can bind one name at two addresses (0x00548790 IsCameraResetting at 0x00548630 v 0x00548B50 at 0x00548690): grade HEAD and read every (target, symbol) pair, not only the ones you plan to change.
- Before replacing a scope-end dtor binding with a member call, scan retail for every call of the target: no unwind-funclet call means an explicit call, not an inline dtor (the inline form matched too but left an ownerless COMDAT).
- `grep -oP "Source = '..."` also matches TestSource: anchor it (`^\s*Source`) or cp gets two paths.
- The Ego_r.symbols.txt dumps carry demangled procs only; mangled spellings need `llvm-pdbutil dump -publics` (the Ego_d publics stream is empty: use its TYPE records).
- Held-binding witness rows of a landing count as the other agent's rows: half the queue lines are deferred by them, so read the landing's held list before planning.
