# NF235 lessons (one line each)
- Porting a vtable emitter onto a full header grows its vtables from the local model's 1 slot to the retail count: every slot row whose self leaf is a stand-in joins the family (count the slot rows before the emitters).
- q.py's SRC_FAIL print is the last 3,000 bytes of cl output: the first error can be cut off; compile vc71(src) yourself and read the head (here a stray `class X {` line copied from a HEAD header).
- A header's `FABLE_STATIC_ASSERT` (non-_AT) carries __LINE__ in its typedef name: any line inserted above it must be followed by pin_asserts, or every includer's preprocessed text changes.
- Reset(0) over the Data-first RELEASEDATA body gives the Info-then-Data store order after VC7.1 folds the dead first Data store: the release-in-a-loop shape retail shows.
- An /O2 TU emits pick-any copies of every inline it expands; size-mode owners (0x0042918C, 0x0042FC86) need the SIZE_BODY specializations, and a declared-only member (LIST_CLEAR_OUTOFLINE, DELETEDATA_DECL_ONLY) stops a whole chain of copies.
- A member helper the ctor expands may be a real private member (LoadProfile 0x00494410): grep the PDBs for the class before keeping a made-up static.
