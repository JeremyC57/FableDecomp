  declared (overrides, access / const / types from the mangled label), slots past the base's retail slot count must all be X's (new
  virtuals in slot order: CFontDataBankEntry / CStreamingFontDataBankEntry IsLoaded / CanUnload / Unload). The destructor stays implicit
