# Chunk H40c lessons (GOTCHAS-ready one-liners)

- (chunk H40c, 2026-11-11) A one-step eax / ecx / edx rotation residue at the START of an else-arm, with every earlier byte equal, can be a statement retail's source has in BOTH arms: the arm generated first leaves its copy's temporaries in the rotation, then c2's late cross-jump merges the two identical tails and the copy disappears. 0x00E64FB0: both arms of the walk test end with `ParentClass.PlayCutscene("CS_CHICKING_INTRO"); Game->FadeScreenIn();` (retail `jmp 0x00E6743E` from 0x00E67141 into the other arm's copy); written once after the if / else the FadeScreenOut vptr at 0x00E6716A is eax instead of edx. Trace to look for: an arm that ends in a `jmp` to the join where the join's first statements are whole statements both arms could own. Metric: `c2prio --rotation` (hand H40c tools/rot.sh, 40 s, no grade).
- (chunk H40c) "registers holding live values are skipped" by the rotation includes a scratch-register CANDIDATE that is already live: a loop index declared and zeroed before the statement in front of its loop keeps ecx busy there. 0x00D5AE70: `long grade = 0; long i = 0; float score = (float)PMasterData->SkillScore; for (; i < 7; ++i)` gives retail's `mov edx,[esi+18h]` at 0x00D5D2E6 (and the 40 lines of rotation after it); `for (long i = 0; ...)` after the score read gives ecx. Swapping two dead initialisers (`grade = 0`) does nothing: the value must really be live.
- (chunk H40c) Frame-slot permutations are often a VARIABLE COUNT, readable from which values retail keeps at one [esp+N]: three rounds' counters all at [esp+38h] = ONE variable assigned three times (0x00D647F0: three variables pack into the string-temporary slots instead); a slot shared by round 1's middle tick, two unrelated counters and round 2's middle tick while the outer ticks keep their own = the second round's middle tick is a SEPARATE variable (0x00D5AE70: weights 10 / 8 / 5 + 5 instead of 10 / 10 / 8). hand H40b tools/pk2.sh shows the result in 13 s without a grade.
- (chunk H40c) c2 weighs a candidate by sum(w x K x cost) with K = the number of candidates (temporaries, named locals, constants) the block references, so a temporary's priority depends on how much ELSE its block mentions. 0x00D647F0: the Game-vtable temporaries of one conversation line needed 144 to beat the `||` flag word (138) and take ebx (retail 0x00D65915 `mov ebx,[ecx]`, flag word reloaded at 0x00D65961); the macro form has K 11 (132, spilled to [esp+14h], +16 bytes); the same three calls through a block-local `long line = Game->AddNewConversation(...); ...; conversation = line;` have K 12 and the copy coalesces away (no code). `c2prio --blocks` prints K and every share; a "lane register-allocation gap" note is not a diagnosis until that listing has been read.
- (chunk H40c) A "rotation" residue on a vector base (`mov eax,[esp+3Ch]` v `mov edx,[esp+3Ch]`) may be allocator pieces of the `_M_start` CSE, not expression temporaries: check that the site has an entry in `c2prio --rotation` before treating it as one (0x00E6BC50: the two loads have none; `--trace` shows candidate #94 chosen with `costs: {edx: -1}`).
- (chunk H40c) The predicate lever (inline_depth(0) round the row-local predicate ctor + FABLE_RETAIL_CPREDICATEBASE_NOVTABLE) is byte-neutral on rows whose prologue already matches (0x00E40E80, 0x00E4B5B0, 0x00D647F0): read the first hunk of the masked diff before spending the grade.
- (chunk H40c) tools/md.py line counts hide independent causes behind slot noise: filter the hunks that are a pure [esp+N] change (hand H40c tools/mdf.py). 0x00D5AE70's "127 lines, one cause (three slots)" held a second cause (a 30-hunk rotation run) that the filter showed at once.
