- Ego_r 0x005FCF94, FableWin 0x02124B99 and Ego_d tc_inventory.obj identify the exact public void leaf. The native caller has the three counts and the parent-list argument.
- Ego_d names the all_items, haircut, facial_hair and tattoo vectors, count, it, the two separate card scopes and the title and subtitle component pointers. Retail classification order, slots and submenu calls corroborate them. The flags scalar describes the native body-area bitmask.
- Native 0x00589468 calls IsBook and 0x005894A3 calls IsExpression. Both PDBs name these item methods. Native 0x0057FF92 is the same-TU CAppearanceModifierDef specialization. Its visible body changes output-pointer treatment. The two definitions' releases still use different scheduling and frame homes.
