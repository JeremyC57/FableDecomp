Natural grouping of the Y and Z squares in GetAccurateMagnitude leaves four extra scheduler tuples across its two expansions. This recovers the exact native store boundary without changing arithmetic or adding instructions. The ungrouped shared helper caused four identity stores to move early. XY/XZ grouped probes moved the boundary but changed sum ordering; YZ matches both. The new opt-in preserves every prior body and default path.

The independent double-precision oracle uses the geometric axis-angle mapping, not production matrix helpers. Parallel and antiparallel directions intentionally skip rotation as native does. Zero-length source/destination segments are outside the tested valid-input domain; no claim is made for that undefined/degenerate geometry. Native/Od/W3 without/EHsc and GCC64 C++03 pass. Callee doubles assert axis length and call counts; non-output inputs and object state are checked unchanged. All input coordinates are exactly representable dyadic floats.
