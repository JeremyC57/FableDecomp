# Chunk 1021 lessons (GOTCHAS-ready)

- (chunk 1021, 0x00A47D10) In an erase-while-iterating loop, `iterator position = it; --it; v.erase(position);` reproduces retail; `v.erase(it--)` (same semantics) lets VC7.1 strength-reduce the inlined erase's `position + 1` into a second induction pointer.
- (chunk 1021, 0x0076D2F0) Out-parameters whose address escapes (`CalculateEnemyTC(thing, &tc, other, ...)`) keep their own frame words for the whole function; declaring them in an inner block and copying them to outer locals lets the frame packer reuse those words. `bool e = a || b;` keeps the result in bl; the FableWin `PeekTC(&ptc)` form fixes the temp's register.
- (chunk 1021, 0x0066BC10) A named `CCharString s = a + ";CS_" + b; v.push_back(s);` (the temporary dies before the push) keeps that push_back expanded where `v.push_back(a + ...)` does not; GetSubDef<T> visible but `__declspec(noinline)` (COMDAT 0x0065F604) keeps the def pointer in edi.
- (chunk 1021, 0x00BEC680) A visible `map::operator[]` (FABLE_RETAIL_STLP_MAP) plus one `key` variable shared by all switch cases homes the key in the dead enum-argument slot; a cached `const C3DMesh2* p = mesh.Get()` in the loop gives retail's spilled `&MaterialCount`.
- (chunk 1021, 0x0083D460) The catalog row was 336 bytes for a 247-byte function (`ret 4` at 0x0083D554, int3 pad, four small functions after): check the body end before grading a long row.
- (chunk 1021, 0x00A0C570) `RHSet.Set(up, forward)` keeps `&RHSet` in esi and forms `&Forward` late (`lea edx,[esi+0Ch]`); `RHSet.SetUp(up); RHSet.SetForward(forward);` makes c2 CSE `this+0x18` with the earlier `GetForward()` reference into edi at entry. C3DVector::InitialiseFromAngles(xy, yz, 1.0f) folds the length away.
- (chunk 1021, 0x006B3E60) A function-static vector is global storage: its `end()` is reloaded after every call unless the source caches it (`for (it = v.begin(), end = v.end(); ...)`); a visible no-capture insert/clear body does not change that.
- (chunk 1021, 0x006B3E60) Expanding a same-TU out-of-line member through its visible body (CEnvironment::GetLocalEnvironmentThemeIndexFromGlobal, out of line at 0x006B2C50) adds a return-value copy that raises the caller's local priority (88 vs 80 in c2prio) and changes its register (edi -> ecx); calling the expanded callee (`DefinitionManager->GetDefClassIndexFromGlobalIndex(...)`) directly gives the wrong colouring.
- (chunk 1021, 0x0079AA90) Moving a `float angle = 0.0f;` declaration after an x87 length computation moves its zero store out of the scheduling region: declared first, dir.Z is spilled `fstp [m] ; fld [m] ; fmul [m]`; declared after, retail's `fst [m] ; fmul [m]` (0x0079AAC8).
- (chunk 1021, 0x0079AA90) `float len = v.GetMagnitude(); v /= len;` homes the length in a dead argument slot and its copy in the reciprocal's slot as retail does; a local `Normalise()` returning the length swaps the two slots.
