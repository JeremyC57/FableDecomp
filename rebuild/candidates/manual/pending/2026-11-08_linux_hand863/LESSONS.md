# Chunk 863 lessons (GOTCHAS one-liners)

- (chunk 863) A while-loop VC7.1 must not rotate: `bool more = true; while (more) { if (c) {...} else more = false; }` gives retail's `c; je exit; body; jmp top`. `for(;;){if(!c)break;}`, `if(c){..continue;}break;` and goto all still rotate (0x00ECE7F0 TakeObject loop).
- (chunk 863) Under wine, cl.exe hangs indefinitely on a missing `#include` (even with /Zs) instead of erroring. Check header paths before grading: CCharString lives in fable_string.h, and there is no engine/CCharString.h.
- (chunk 863) A root-level header (fable_string.h, `#pragma once`) cannot be overlaid from hdr/: repo headers that include it resolve the repo copy first, so the hdr copy gives C2011 redefinitions. Put its debt in NOTES, not in an overlay.
- (chunk 863) A local template stand-in for CIntelligentPointer must be `class`, not `struct`, or the leaf mangles U instead of V. A local template GetTC must be `__forceinline`, or VC7.1 calls it out of line.
- (chunk 863) Declaring `template<> CCountedPointer<Movie>::~CCountedPointer();` (out of line, 0x006E7AB0) makes the movie dtor cheaper, so more MovieBase dtor sites expand. Adding a row-local `inline ~CScriptThing() {}` then fixes a single expanded temporary dtor (0x00ECE7F0, md 299 -> 4).
- (chunk 863) `mov eax,esp; push eax` as the hidden return pointer of a by-value conversion argument (0x00ECF317, CDefString -> CCharString) appears only twice in the exe (here and in libvorbis). 40 C++ spellings all give `push esp`.
- (chunk 863) The ~CTimer (DeregisterTimer) expansion probes on 0x00ECBDF0: with no return inside a pause (CWideScreenMagicPauseEntities) scope, VC7.1 expands ~CTimer nowhere. Removing pause-exit returns one by one moves the single expansion to the last remaining pause exit. A single do-while(0) at a plain thread check adds an expansion there but does not remove the pause-exit one.
- (chunk 863) `#pragma inline_depth(0)` around a return also outlines a `__forceinline` dtor (the pause dtor) at that return, and changed the frame size (0xDC -> 0xE0). It cannot split two dtors at one exit. `inline_depth(1)` does not stop a nested plain-inline member dtor inside a `__forceinline` dtor.
- (chunk 863) /d2inlT has cliffs, not a slope: 0x00ECBDF0 keeps both ~CTimer expansions at T41+ and has none at T38-40. On 0x00D11930, T75-78 is identical to the default.
- (chunk 863) ~CIsThingAlive at a SetPredicate temporary: retail expands it in some TUs, re-storing the vtable from edi and calling ~CScriptThing 0x004AA840 out of line (0x00D11930, landed 0x00D6A360). Other TUs call 0x00E241B0 (0x00ECE7F0). The expansion also moves the temporary's frame slot. In a TU that needs ~CScriptThing inline elsewhere, neither plain inline, `__forceinline` nor an implicit dtor gives "outer expanded, nested called".
- (chunk 863) The `push -1; lea ecx,[esp+X]; push "lit"; call CCharString` interleave (the lea between the pushes) occurs naturally in landed rows 0x00DCC613 and 0x00E58B8B. At 0x00DD0338 (0x00DCFA60, after a cross-jump merge point) no spelling reproduced it.
