## Naming recovery and its limits

The original names available in donor PDB exports and generated class layouts include function/class/template names, signatures and many member names. Native RTTI, call relationships, literals and field access patterns establish which donor name belongs to the native body. Generated manifest labels are often propagated from byte-identical unrelated functions; they are hints, not proof. Original local-variable names generally have not been recovered. The archive indexes descriptive local/helper names separately and does not present them as original source.

Some native layouts differ because STL containers have12-byte native representations versus16-byte donor representations, or because editor-only virtual methods exist in donor layouts. Keep original member spellings when proved, but distinguish original names from a reconstructed native offset association. A PDB spelling co-occurrence is weaker than independently verified native identity. names_audit records exact evidence locations and unresolved cases rather than converting every matching string into a recovered original name.

Examples requiring care include actual ScrollMenu scrollbar IsDragged (not IsScrolling), long GetGlobalIndexFromClassIndex(...,1) (not a bool name lookup), CONFIG_OPTIONS_DEFAULTS_DEF (not IconsDef), original GFSerialiseMapBinaryOut<CHeroStatIncreaseData,EDamageAttribute> (not a propagated WriteToStream label), and CIsThingConscious versus CIsThingAlive. Native table1238C6C identifies CIntelligentPointer<CThing> even though a propagated deleting-destructor row says landscape. Full details and remaining unknowns are in the names index and root reviews.
